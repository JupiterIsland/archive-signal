About Archive Signal

Why Preservation Matters

Archive Signal was created by Richard John Murray from a lifelong interest in music history, 1980s and 1990s broadcast culture, and the recordings that risk being forgotten.

Every year, interviews, documentaries, radio programmes, live performances, and television broadcasts disappear from public view through rights changes, platform closures, ageing magnetic tape, and forgotten collections.

Archive Signal exists to document, preserve, and provide historical context for those recordings—ensuring that important pieces of cultural history remain discoverable for future generations.

Because preservation is about more than saving media. It's about preserving the stories attached to it.
